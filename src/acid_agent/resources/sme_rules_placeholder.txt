# SME Business Rules Placeholder for Acid Job Detection
# Replace this with your production 500-line SME prompt when available.

## Objective
1. Identify whether a sentence describes an acid job event.
2. Classify event into one of four subtypes:
   - matrix_acidizing
   - acid_fracturing
   - acid_wash
   - acid_spearhead
3. Avoid false positives from chemical inventory notes without treatment action.

## Global Interpretation Rules
- Treat the daily report as a chronological operating log.
- Prioritize direct operational action verbs (pumped, performed, stimulated).
- If text only states material on location, do not count as a job.
- If text says planned/future, do not count until execution evidence exists.
- Require at least one acid indicator plus one action indicator.
- Combine contiguous sentences as one event when they refer to same operation.

## Subtype Definitions
- matrix_acidizing: near-wellbore acid stimulation without intentional fracture propagation.
- acid_fracturing: acid treatment with fracture initiation/propagation intent.
- acid_wash: wash/cleanup/scale-removal focused acid circulation or soak.
- acid_spearhead: spearhead/preflush/pad acid stage preceding a larger treatment.

## Synonym Dictionary
- matrix acid job = matrix_acidizing
- acid frac / acid-frac = acid_fracturing
- tubing wash with acid = acid_wash
- pad acid / preflush / spearhead acid = acid_spearhead

## Evidence Quality Heuristics
- Strong evidence: operation verb + acid chemical + volume/rate/pressure details.
- Medium evidence: operation verb + acid mention but limited numeric context.
- Weak evidence: acid mention with ambiguous action; review with caution.

## Precision Rules
- Do not infer acid job from generic word 'stimulation' unless acid is explicit.
- Do not infer from pressure test, drillout, flowback, or rig activity unless acid action is explicit.
- If multiple acid stages occur same day, count each stage only if log separates them as distinct jobs.
